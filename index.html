Good to know

What is Milo’s Cafe known for?

Focaccia sandwiches, espresso, and sweet and savory crepes. News & Observer readers voted Milo’s the Best Sandwich Shop in the Triangle in 2026. Read the coverage.

Is there a good coffee shop in Morrisville, NC?

Yes. Milo’s Cafe is a European-style coffee shop at 10119 Chapel Hill Rd in Morrisville, serving Lavazza espresso, cappuccinos, cortados, matcha and seasonal specialty lattes, with indoor seating and a shaded patio.

Is Milo’s Cafe close to Cary?

Yes. Milo’s Cafe is on Chapel Hill Rd (NC 54) in Morrisville, a few minutes’ drive from Cary, so it’s an easy stop for coffee, breakfast or lunch if you live or work in Cary.

Where can I get a great sandwich in Morrisville or Cary?

Milo’s Cafe in Morrisville, minutes from Cary, makes focaccia sandwiches with bread from Ninth Street Bakery, like the schnitzel, salami, prosciutto and smoked salmon focaccia. News & Observer readers voted Milo’s the Best Sandwich Shop in the Triangle in 2026.

Where is Milo’s Cafe?

Milo’s Cafe is at 10119 Chapel Hill Rd, Morrisville, NC 27560.

What does Milo’s Cafe serve?

Lavazza espresso and coffee drinks, matcha, specialty lattes and teas, plus focaccia sandwiches, salads, toast and bowls, and sweet or savory crepes, all made from scratch.

Can I order online for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order through our online ordering page for pickup, curbside or delivery, or come in and dine with us.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. We have indoor seating and a patio with bistro chairs and shaded tables.

What are your opening hours?

Monday–Thursday: 7 AM–4 PM
Friday–Saturday: 7 AM–5 PM
Sunday: 8:30 AM–3:30 PM
Holiday hours may vary.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes, including avocado toast, goat cheese toast, veggie focaccia and several crepes. Please ask us about ingredients and allergens.
