How to edit the happy’s site

Everything on the site, from hours to the menu, flyers, photos and reviews, can be changed here without touching any code.

The basics

  1. On the left, open Site content and pick the section you want to change.
  2. Change what you need. The preview on the right shows the real site with your changes as you type, scrolled to the part you’re editing.
  3. When it looks right, click Publish at the top, then Publish now.
  4. The live site updates in about a minute. Refresh the site to see it.
Nothing you type goes live until you publish. If you change your mind, leave the page without publishing; the CMS will ask before it throws your changes away.

To make the editing area wider, hide the preview with the eye icon at the top of the preview, then click it again to bring the preview back.

Closing for a private event

This puts a big red “closed for a private event” notice across the top of the site and above the hours, and says when you’re back open.

  1. Open Closed for a private event, the first item in Site content.
  2. Turn on Show the notice.
  3. Set Back open at to the day and time you reopen (Cary time).
  4. Closed from is optional. Leave it empty if you’re closed right now. Set it if you want to post a heads-up ahead of time, and the notice will say “heads up · closed saturday from 4pm”.
  5. Change the headline or add an extra line if you like (for example: “want the place to yourself? email us”).
  6. Publish.
The notice takes itself down once the reopen time passes, so nobody has to remember to switch it off. Turn Show the notice off whenever you want to clear it out of the editor.

Hours

  • Open Hours. List only the days you’re open; any day left out shows as closed.
  • Write times like 4pm, 11:30pm or 12am (midnight).
  • The hours table, the footer, today’s highlight and Google’s listing all update from this one list.
  • The small note under the hours (like “(for now)”) is optional.

Menu

  • Open Menu → Menu sections. Click a section to open it, then Add items at the bottom of its list to add a drink.
  • Price is just the number, like 10. Leave it empty when the section shows one price for everything (the “price next to the title”).
  • Drag the handle on the left of a section or item to reorder it. Click the × on an item to remove it.
  • Filter button it belongs to decides which filter button (cocktails, beer, wine…) shows the section.
  • Colors are picked by name, so every card stays on-brand.

Music flyers

  1. Open Music flyers → Add flyer.
  2. Pick the month, upload the flyer (the same image you post on Instagram) and give it a heading like “december music”.
  3. Publish. That’s all it needs.

Optional: type in the shows too (date, who’s playing, style). Then they list as text on the page, past dates cross out, the next one is marked, and Google can list them as events.

Add next month’s flyer whenever it’s ready. The site shows this month’s flyer, switches to the next one once this month’s shows are done, and never shows a month that’s over.

Photos

  • Photos is the “lately at happy’s” strip. Five fills the row.
  • Use square photos at least 900px wide. Phone photos are fine; crop them square first.
  • Click a photo field to upload a new one or pick one you’ve uploaded before.
  • Fill in the description for screen readers in a sentence (“bartender pouring an orange crush”). It helps people using screen readers, and Google.

Everything else

  • Events: the event cards. Turn on Show this month’s music flyer on the live music card. Remove every card to hide the section.
  • Reviews: star rating, review count, tags and quotes. Keep quotes short and word-for-word.
  • Private events page: photos, “what works here”, the steps and the text around the inquiry form on the private events page.
  • Contact, hero & the rest: address, phone, email, Instagram, the big headline and background photo at the top, the storefront photo, the email signup text and the footer.

Not in here: the page layout, the inquiry form’s questions, and the title and description Google shows. Ask Draft and Logic for those.

Fixing a mistake

  • Spotted it before publishing? Change it back, or leave without publishing.
  • Already published? Change it back and publish again.
  • Something broke and you can’t tell why? Every publish is saved in the site’s history, so Draft and Logic can roll back any change. And if an edit would break the site, the update is refused and the last good version stays live.
Changes can take a minute to show. If the live site still looks old, wait a moment and refresh before trying again.
